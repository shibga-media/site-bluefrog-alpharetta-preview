Emergency Plumbing in Alpharetta, GA and Nearby

Burst pipes, major leaks, overflowing toilets and backups.

Call (678) 331-5843Contact us

Sudden plumbing failures can create urgent and messy situations in your home. Homeowners in the Alpharetta, Georgia area call Bluefrog Plumbing + Drain of Alpharetta to handle unexpected bursts or major backups.

If you smell gas, leave the home immediately and call the local emergency number from a safe distance. For other issues, shut off the water at the main valve if it is safe to do so. The plumber will locate the issue and contain the water to minimize damage to your property.

Locate your main water shutoff valve now so you know where it is during an emergency. Take clear photos of the damage for your records before cleanup begins.

It helps to have a few details ready when you call: where in the house the problem is, when you first noticed it, and whether it is getting worse. If water is involved, say whether you have been able to shut it off. You do not need to know the cause or use plumbing terms. A plain description of what you see, hear or smell is enough to get started.
